A private production tool for our own YouTube story channels

StoryFactory writes, narrates, illustrates and edits long-form dramatized stories, then publishes them to the YouTube channels owned and operated by its developer.

Uses YouTube API Services

StoryFactory uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, only on channels owned by its operator, to:

  • upload finished videos as private and schedule their publication time;
  • set titles, descriptions, tags, language, the altered or synthetic content disclosure, thumbnails, captions and playlists;
  • apply channel branding (description, keywords and banner) and create playlists;
  • read the channels' own performance reports (views, watch time, subscribers) to plan the next videos.

By using StoryFactory you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses it

StoryFactory is not a public service and has no sign-up. It runs on its operator's own computer and is used only by its operator, Fernando Rampon, to manage his own channels. Every video is dramatized fiction or history narration, clearly labeled in its description as created with the help of AI, and declared through YouTube's altered or synthetic content setting.
